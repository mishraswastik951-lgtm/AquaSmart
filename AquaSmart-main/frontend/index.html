1

Configure Wi-Fi

2

Install Libraries

Install ArduinoJson and HTTPClient libraries in Arduino IDE.

3

Wire Sensors

Connect soil moisture probe to ADC Pin 34 and DHT22 to a Digital Pin.

AquaSmart

Smart Watering for Better Yields

AquaSmart helps you water your fields exactly when needed, saving water and improving your crop yields. Our smart system looks at weather, temperature, and soil data to make automatic decisions tailored to your specific crops.

Water Savings

Only irrigate when your soil needs it. Our AI learns your field's patterns to prevent overwatering.

Crop Specific

Whether you grow Rice, Maize, or Mustard, the system adjusts its moisture targets to give your plants exactly what they need.

Easy Sensors

Plug and play support for soil moisture and temperature sensors right in your fields.

See exactly what's happening in your fields right now from your phone or computer.
